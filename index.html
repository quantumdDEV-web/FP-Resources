SYSTEM INFORMATION

Built by students. Open to everyone.

FP Library is an independent, student-built public resource website created to help Physics students at Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Larache find, organize, and share useful academic materials.

This website is not officially owned, operated, approved, or affiliated with Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Larache, Université Abdelmalek Essaâdi, or any university administration.

The platform is made by students for students. Its purpose is to support learning through shared course notes, summaries, exercises, tutorials, documents, and other educational resources related to Physics.

Students may contribute by uploading and sharing documents that could help other students with their studies. Uploaded materials should be relevant, respectful, and used for educational purposes.

The website is publicly accessible on the internet. Anyone may visit and view the available resources, while the main intended community is Physics students at FP Larache.

Important notice: FP Library is an independent student initiative. It does not represent the university or speak on behalf of its staff, departments, or administration.
